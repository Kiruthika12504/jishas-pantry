Privacy policy

Last updated:

We're a small kitchen, and we'd rather tell you plainly what happens to your information than hide it in legal language. Here's all of it.

What we collect

When you order, only what we need to get food to you:

  • your name and mobile number
  • your delivery address, pincode and landmark
  • what you ordered, when you want it, how you'll pay, and any cooking notes

If you create an account (optional), we also keep your email address and the delivery details you choose to save. Your password is stored scrambled (hashed) by our login provider. Nobody at our kitchen can see it.

We don't ask for your date of birth, card details or anything else. We will never ask for an OTP, password or card number — not on the phone, not on WhatsApp.

How your order reaches us

When you press "Send order on WhatsApp", your order is written into a WhatsApp message, and it reaches us only when you press send yourself. WhatsApp is run by Meta, and that message is covered by WhatsApp's privacy policy.

Where account details are stored

Accounts and saved delivery details are stored with Supabase, our database provider, on servers in Mumbai, India. Security rules in the database let each customer read and change only their own details. Our kitchen team can see them only to deliver your orders.

What we use it for

  • cooking and delivering your order
  • calling or messaging you about that order (for example, if we can't find your address)
  • filling in your details at checkout, if you have an account
  • keeping the billing records the law requires us to keep

We won't send you offers or promotions unless you ask us to.

Who else sees it

The person delivering your food sees your name, number and address so they can reach you. Our database provider stores account data on our behalf and can't use it for anything else. We never sell, rent or trade your information.

How long we keep it

Account details stay until you delete your account. Order details are kept only as long as we need them to deliver your order and to keep the tax and accounting records Indian law requires.

What stays on your own device

Your cart and your light/dark theme choice are saved in your browser. If you're logged in, a login token is saved there too, so you stay logged in. If you tick "Remember my details on this device" as a guest, your name, number and address are saved in your browser as well. None of this is used for tracking.

No tracking

This website uses no analytics, no advertising pixels and no tracking cookies. We don't know who visits it, and nor does anyone else through us.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. If you have an account, you can edit or delete it yourself on the account page. Otherwise, just contact us below — we'll reply within 7 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
