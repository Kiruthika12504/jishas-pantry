Why we started

Most of these recipes came from my mother's kitchen. The biryani is hers, the kothu parotta is from the street stall near our old house, and the curd rice is exactly how she packed it for school.

When we moved to the city, everything we ordered tasted the same: too oily, too rushed, or reheated. So we started cooking for friends, then for their friends, and this kitchen grew from there.

We keep the menu small on purpose. Ten dishes we can cook properly every single day, rather than fifty we'd have to cut corners on.

— The family behind the kitchen

How ordering works

No app, no sign-up. Your order comes straight to us.

  1. Pick your dishes

    Add what you like to the cart. Choose "as soon as possible" or a later time slot.

  2. Send it on WhatsApp

    Your order opens in WhatsApp, already written out. Press send and a real person in our kitchen replies to confirm.

  3. We cook, then deliver

    Your food is cooked after you order, packed in sealed containers and brought to your door.

How do I pay?

By UPI or cash when your food arrives. We never ask for card details, OTPs or passwords — if anyone claiming to be us does, it isn't us.

Is there a minimum order?

Can I order for later?

Yes. At checkout, pick a time slot for later today or tomorrow.

Something's wrong with my order. What now?

Message us on WhatsApp with a photo. If we got it wrong, we'll replace the dish or refund you — no long back-and-forth.

What do you do with my details?

We use your name, number and address only to deliver your order. We don't sell or share them, and this website has no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
