Ordering

  • You place an order by sending it to us on WhatsApp from this website. It's confirmed only when we reply to confirm it.
  • Prices on the menu are per plate. Packaging, delivery and GST are shown separately before you send the order, and the total you see is what you pay.
  • We deliver only to the areas listed on the website, during kitchen hours.
  • Payment is by UPI or cash on delivery. We never ask for card numbers, OTPs or passwords.
  • Dishes are freshly cooked and may contain milk, nuts, gluten, egg or other allergens. Tell us about allergies in the order notes and we'll tell you honestly what we can and can't guarantee.

Cancellations and refunds

  • Before we start cooking: message or call us to cancel. There's no charge.
  • After cooking has started: we may not be able to cancel, because the food is made for you. We'll tell you before charging anything.
  • Wrong, missing, spoiled or poor-quality food: send us a photo on WhatsApp within 2 hours of delivery. We'll replace the dish or give a full refund for it, your choice.
  • Very late delivery: if your order arrives more than 30 minutes after the time we confirmed, tell us and we'll refund the delivery charge.
  • Refunds go back the way you paid: UPI refunds within 5 working days; cash orders are refunded by UPI or on your next order, your choice.
  • We don't charge you any cancellation fee that we wouldn't bear ourselves.
